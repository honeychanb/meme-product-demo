Salary Rules

Estimated Salary = Target Salary + Diamond Salary.

Lv1–Lv9: Target Salary is calculated at 70% until both 4 Valid Days and 10 Valid Working Hours are completed; then it is calculated at 100% for the period.

Lv10+ is exempt from Working Time requirements and receives 100% Target Salary. Diamond Salary is not reduced by Working Time and is converted at 25,000 Diamonds = $1.

The 100% amount uses the current period's data and may change. Estimated Salary is not the final settlement. Last Period uses the settled Final Salary, including adjustments where applicable.

Coins

Lucky Gift Coins are counted at 10%.

Up-mic Time

Only time when the host is on mic with the microphone turned on is counted.

Valid Days

At least 2 hours of valid up-mic time in one day counts as 1 Valid Day.

For Lv1–Lv9, the current period target is 4 Valid Days + 10 Valid Working Hours.
